## English

### Data we collect and why

HBT may collect account identity and phone details; passenger identity
including NRC or alternative documents; booking, seat, trip and ticket data;
manual-payment evidence and references; cargo sender, receiver, item and
custody data; device, offline-sync and notification data; support, feedback,
audit and security logs. We use it to provide passenger and cargo operations,
verify payments, issue invoices/refunds, synchronize offline work, prevent
fraud, support customers, report to authorized operators and comply with law.

NRC values are designed to be encrypted at rest with a blind index for
duplicate checks. Decryption is restricted to approved operational purposes.

### Sharing

We share only what is needed with the relevant transport operator and crew,
authorized company approvers, contracted infrastructure/payment/notification/
security providers, and competent authorities where legally required. HBT does
not sell personal data.

### Retention, security and rights

The production schedule remains `[RETENTION SCHEDULE TO BE APPROVED]`. Data is
deleted or de-identified after applicable legal, accounting and dispute periods.
Controls include encryption, tenant isolation, least privilege, audit logging,
malware scanning, backups and incident response, but no system is risk-free.

Subject to applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion or
restriction, withdraw consent and complain. Some ticket, payment and security
records cannot be deleted immediately where retention or fraud-prevention duties
apply. Requests require proportionate identity verification.

Children's passenger data must be supplied by a guardian or authorized booking
party. Material policy changes receive an updated date and notice. HBT will
handle and notify eligible data incidents under applicable law and its incident
plan.
