## English

HBT is a SaaS platform connecting passengers and transport businesses through
booking, ticketing, Cargo Lite, payment-record, operations and reporting tools.
The transport company shown on the ticket is the carrier. Unless HBT is
expressly identified as the carrier, HBT does not itself operate the journey.

Users must provide accurate account, passenger, identity, cargo and payment
information; protect passwords, OTPs and devices; and not forge tickets, ship
prohibited goods, commit fraud, attack or scrape the service, or access another
tenant. Shared company logins are prohibited.

The relevant operator controls fares, seats, cargo acceptance, departure,
cancellation, refund, reissue and delay policies. Applicable quotes and policies
must be shown before checkout or approval. A manual payment becomes confirmed
only after authorized operator verification; uploading a screenshot alone does
not issue a ticket. Refunds remain subject to operator policy, the confirmed
payment ceiling and the approval lifecycle.

An e-ticket may be saved, shared or printed, but copies do not create additional
tickets. Server-side validation, revocation and reissue state controls use.
Offline operations may conflict or be rejected when the server rechecks current
authorization and domain state.

Business customers manage authorized users, roles, counters, devices and
approvers. Fees, tax, limits, renewal, grace/suspension and add-ons must be
defined in the order form. HBT may restrict access for security, maintenance,
law or misuse while handling export and recovery under the applicable plan and
law.
