# AI Prompt — Users, Roles, and Use Cases

Act as a transportation operations expert, product manager, UX researcher, business analyst, and security architect.

Create the authoritative **Users, Roles, Personas, and Use-Case Catalog** for HBT.

Read the existing business, module, workflow, and security documents first. Convert their guidance into a consistent catalog.

## Include

1. Internal and external user categories
2. Tenant owner and platform operator distinction
3. Company, branch, terminal, operations, sales, finance, driver, conductor, and passenger personas
4. Goals, responsibilities, pain points, devices, connectivity constraints, and skill levels
5. Role definitions and prohibited responsibilities
6. Primary, alternate, exception, and recovery use cases
7. Actor-to-use-case matrix
8. Role-to-module access matrix
9. Branch and tenant data-scope expectations
10. Approval and segregation-of-duty requirements
11. Accessibility and localization considerations
12. Persona assumptions requiring stakeholder validation

For every use case provide:

- Identifier and name
- Primary and secondary actors
- Trigger
- Preconditions
- Main flow
- Alternate flows
- Failure and recovery flows
- Postconditions
- Business rules
- Required permissions
- Audit events
- Related module and workflow
- Acceptance criteria

Do not invent organization-specific job titles as confirmed facts. Clearly label recommendations and open questions.
