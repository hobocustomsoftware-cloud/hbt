# AI Prompt — Measurable Non-Functional Requirements

Act as an enterprise architect, SRE, security architect, performance engineer, accessibility specialist, and QA architect.

Create measurable **Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)** for the HBT MVP and its expected growth stages.

Do not use vague words such as fast, scalable, secure, reliable, or user-friendly without a measurable target or an explicitly marked decision gap.

## Define measurable requirements for

1. Availability and planned maintenance
2. Response time and throughput by critical journey
3. Concurrent users, tenants, trips, bookings, tickets, and devices
4. Offline duration, local storage, synchronization latency, and conflict tolerance
5. Data durability, backup, RPO, and RTO
6. Security, privacy, tenancy isolation, encryption, and auditability
7. Accessibility and supported interaction modes
8. Localization, languages, time zones, currency, and number/date handling
9. Device, browser, mobile OS, and network support
10. Observability, alerting, diagnostics, and supportability
11. Maintainability, testability, deployability, and upgradeability
12. Data retention, archival, deletion, and export
13. Integration reliability and rate limits
14. AI safety, latency, cost, fallback, and human oversight where applicable

For every NFR provide ID, scenario, metric, target, measurement method, environment, priority, owner, and validation stage.

Where business targets are unknown, propose a clearly labeled baseline and ask for approval. Include an NFR test matrix and capacity assumptions.
